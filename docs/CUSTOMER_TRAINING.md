# Synthetic onboarding and training / Syntetisk onboarding og træning

English: Use `/help` and `/integrations` with fictional local accounts. First agree
roles, read-only scope and independent contingency. Review inventory placement and
calibration evidence, then QA thresholds/cadence and explicitly TEST-only routes.
Exercise missing data, outage/replay and failed delivery; absence of alarms never
proves food safety. Acknowledge and investigate an incident, append corrective
action, verify recovery and obtain independent QA review before closure. Generate
an authorized report, inspect gaps/provenance and retain its acceptance reference.
Submit/resolve a synthetic support request: this does not close the safety incident
or contact a supplier. Owner billing shows usage and manual drafts only. Switch
language and verify current site scope after login/logout and permission changes.

Dansk: Brug `/help` og `/integrations` med fiktive lokale konti. Aftal roller,
skrivebeskyttet omfang og selvstændigt beredskab. Gennemgå placering og kalibrering,
derefter QA-grænser, datarytme og udtrykkelige TEST-ruter. Afprøv manglende data,
udfald/genafspilning og leveringsfejl; fravær af alarmer beviser aldrig fødevaresikkerhed.
Kvittér og undersøg en hændelse, tilføj korrigerende handling, verificér genopretning
og få uafhængig QA-gennemgang før lukning. Opret en godkendt rapport, gennemgå huller
og oprindelse, og bevar acceptreferencen. Opret/afslut en syntetisk supportanmodning:
det lukker ikke sikkerhedshændelsen og kontakter ingen leverandør. Ejerens
faktureringsside viser kun forbrug og manuelle kladder. Skift sprog og kontrollér
lokationsadgang efter login/logout og rettighedsændringer.

Record attendance, named customer QA/on-call responsibility, demonstrated failure
drills and signed acceptance only after separately authorized actual training.
This template is not a training completion record. No physical pilot, real contacts,
contractual response promise or supplier qualification is inferred from local tests.
